# ESP32-CAM Camera Streaming

This was the first development stage of the project.

The purpose of this program was to configure the **ESP32-CAM** and verify that the camera could successfully capture and stream images over Wi-Fi before moving on to the machine learning stage.

### Objectives

* Configure the ESP32-CAM camera.
* Establish Wi-Fi connectivity.
* Create a live camera stream.
* Verify that the camera was functioning correctly.

### Outcome

The camera was successfully configured and able to provide a live stream. This confirmed that the ESP32-CAM hardware was ready for the subsequent dataset collection and object detection stages.
